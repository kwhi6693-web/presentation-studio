# Design Systems

## Swiss

Use Guizang's registered Swiss layouts, grid, IKB color logic, thin typographic hierarchy, image-slot contracts, and slide rhythm. Assign a registered `data-layout` and keep body titles on the intended left/top axis.

## Editorial

Use strong type scale, asymmetric composition, deliberate whitespace, image-led spreads, and varied adjacent silhouettes. Alternate density and role across cover, argument, evidence, comparison, process, quote, section, and conclusion slides.

## General quality

Avoid repeated cards, excessive rounded rectangles, purple-blue AI gradients, giant title plus tiny panels, repeated three-column grids, decorative icons without meaning, emoji icons, fake charts, generic stock imagery, and identical hero layouts.

When converting the visual system to editable PPTX, preserve its hierarchy and rhythm with native objects; do not flatten it into a screenshot.
